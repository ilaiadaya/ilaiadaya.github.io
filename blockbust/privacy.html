BLOCK BUST

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Block Bust (“the app”) is a block puzzle game published by Ilai Bachrach. This policy explains what data the app handles.

What we collect

We don’t ask for or store any account, name, email or contact details. Your scores, settings and game progress are saved only on your device.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • Your device’s advertising identifier (on iPhone only if you allow tracking in Apple’s prompt)
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device type, operating system and app version
  • Information about the ads you see and tap

If you’re in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google’s consent tool. You can change your choice at any time from the game’s settings. On iPhone you can also turn tracking off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Google’s use of this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Children

The app isn’t directed at children under 13 and we don’t knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Because we don’t store personal data ourselves, there is nothing for us to export or delete. For data held by Google you can use My Ad Center. Deleting the app removes all game data on your device.

Changes

We’ll update this page if anything changes and change the date above.
