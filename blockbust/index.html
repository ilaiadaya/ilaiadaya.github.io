BLOCK BUST

Support

Block Bust is a free block puzzle game for iPhone. Fill the 8×8 board, clear rows and columns, and chain combos.

Frequently asked

How do I play?

Drag a piece onto the board. A full row or column clears. The game ends when none of your three pieces fit.

How do combos work?

Clear lines on back-to-back moves to build a combo. Clearing several lines at once, or the whole board, scores extra.

What does Revive do?

Once per game, when you’re stuck, you can watch a short video to clear three crowded lines and get pieces that fit.

I lost my progress

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it, and it can’t be restored.

Why do I see ads?

Ads keep the game free. Full-screen ads only appear between games, never while you play.
